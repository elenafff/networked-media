Shanghai, the city I always miss


I grown up here and I've been living there for 18 years. I miss being close to my family and friends, miss the time we spent together all around the city.





Now that I spend most of my time in New York, I think I notice these things even more. New York has gradually become another important place in my life. I have friends and new responsibilities to do. But it feels very different from Shanghai.





Every time I go back to Shanghai, everything somehow feels familiar again, foods, people, and even noises of city...





I think that is why, no matter how many places I travel to or live in, Shanghai will always be the best in my mind.
